Sprites & Data

Fonts

Both fonts are open-source and served via Google Fonts CDN.

Algorithm

Squads are scored by: offensive type coverage (how many of the 18 types your team hits super-effectively), defensive type diversity, average Pokémon level, and starred lock-in preferences. Up to 800 candidate combinations are evaluated instantly in-browser. No data leaves your device.
